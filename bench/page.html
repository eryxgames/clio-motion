One motion core drives all four characters. Nothing on this page is keyframed: feet are planted by a gait rule, joints by inverse kinematics, the rest by springs and gravity.

Turn round works in both views. Steering is easiest to follow in the three-quarter view.

Hit staggers the body and it recovers. Knock down lets it fall as a ragdoll. Both push away from the pointer.

The rider is a second character, stepped after its mount: hips sprung to the saddle, feet in the stirrups, hands on the reins until the rifle is drawn. Then it aims at the pointer and the mouse fires.

Rungs are unevenly spaced on purpose. Each hand and foot finds a real one.

Hands and fists

The grenade is lobbed to the ground under the place last pointed at (or press G while pointing). The crate is lifted from a squat and can be walked about with.

Two soldiers, each run by the same motion core. A short script on top decides when to step in, punch, cover up or back off.
